# Sustainable AI Engineering Roadmap

## Objective

Expand the maintained AI tooling surface with energy-conscious engineering practices while preserving deterministic evidence contracts.

## Engineering priorities

### Efficient execution

- Track meaningful workload signals.
- Prefer efficient model/tool choices.
- Avoid unnecessary repeated computation.

### Evidence quality

Sustainability metadata should remain:

- validated,
- immutable after creation,
- reproducible,
- separate from sensitive operational data.

### Future integrations

Potential additions:

- efficiency scoring utilities,
- workload comparison reports,
- energy-aware acceptance evidence,
- developer diagnostics.

All additions must include tests and maintain existing package boundaries.

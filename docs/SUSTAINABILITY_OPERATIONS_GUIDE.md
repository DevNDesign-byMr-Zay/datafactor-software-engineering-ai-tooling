# Sustainability Operations Guide

## Purpose

The sustainability layer turns energy awareness into measurable engineering signals.

## Current capabilities

- Validate energy execution receipts.
- Track renewable contribution metadata.
- Calculate deterministic efficiency indicators.

## Engineering approach

Every sustainability improvement should remain:

- measurable
- reproducible
- reviewable
- compatible with existing evidence contracts

## Human impact

The goal is smarter software operation: reducing waste while maintaining quality, speed, and trust.
